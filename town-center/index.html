The latest in Town Center

November 12, 2026

The Building 120 hearing on November 17 is about one parcel, 3425069017. The proposal would change its Comprehensive Plan map designation from Neighborhood Residential to Mixed Use Center, which would allow a later rezone from R-6 to Community Business. The council's agenda packet, posted November 10, carries the proposed amendment. Hearing notice

November 5, 2026

Sammamish Commons is a 25-acre park in the middle of town. The Upper Commons at 801 228th Avenue SE holds City Hall, the library, a skate park, a basketball court, play equipment and Commons Plaza. The Lower Commons at 550 222nd Place SE has a community garden, a native plant garden, a looped trail, shelters and a playground. Commons page

October 29, 2026

Construction on the city's half of SE 6th Street, along the north end of Sammamish Commons, is scheduled to last through Friday, October 30, with completion listed for November. The $2.5 million street adds sidewalk, community parking, trees, lighting and stormwater lines, and a new link to the Lower Commons. Project page

October 22, 2026

Sammamish Parks & Recreation runs a free Halloween evening at the Sammamish Commons by City Hall on Thursday, October 29, 4 to 7 PM, with candy, crafts and food trucks. Costumes encouraged. City of Sammamish

October 15, 2026

The SE 6th Street work limits access to the Lower Commons parking lot and paths through mid-October, per the city. Below City Hall, the Lower Commons, 550 222nd Place SE, holds the community and native plant gardens, looped trails, shelters and the spray park, part of the 25-acre Sammamish Commons. Sammamish Commons

October 12, 2026

The city's half of SE 6th Street, along the north end of Sammamish Commons between 222nd Place SE and 224th Avenue SE, is under construction through October 30: sidewalk, community parking, trees, lighting and stormwater. On the same block, Pumpkins on the Plaza runs Thursday, October 29, 4 to 7 PM, free, on the Upper Commons. SE 6th Street · Pumpkins on the Plaza Also on 228th: Building 120, the former university building at 120 228th Ave NE, is Thursday's hearing. A city review found it structurally sound with aging systems; the second phase of community engagement is paused, and the building is not available for rentals. Building 120

Questions people ask

Is this a real estate newsletter?

No. It is a Sammamish newsletter that happens to know the housing market. Three of the four sections are about living here. The fourth tells you what homes are selling for, because in Sammamish that is news too.

Who writes it?

Neighborhood Press LLC, an independent publisher. Every item comes from Sammamish's own public record (council agendas, permit notices, school district and transit updates, parks calendars) and links its source. It is researched and written with AI tools, and an editor reads every issue before it goes out.

How often, and is it really free?

Once a week, Thursday at 6 AM, about five minutes to read. Free, no paywall, unsubscribe in one click at the bottom of any issue.

Who owns it?

Neighborhood Press LLC, an independent publishing company. The newsletter is free, and no sponsor buys coverage. Our standards.

What happens with my address?

It is used for one thing: a monthly estimate of what your home would sell for today. It goes to a local real estate brokerage under the same ownership as our publisher only if you tick the sharing box on the form; that brokerage may then contact you about your home. Leave the box empty and it stays with us. Never sold or posted. Remove it any time by replying to any issue.
