What the city decided and what changed

The Planning Commission takes up a new zoning plan for the Town Center tonight at 6:30 PM

Staff propose replacing the Town Center's five zones (TC-A to TC-E) with the city's regular ones, Community Business in the core stepping down to R-18 and R-12 toward the neighborhoods, and repealing…

Tuesday's council agenda carried the 2027–28 budget, the 2027 property tax levy, the six-year capital plan, the school impact fees and the Building 120 map change, each up for adoption

We went to press before the votes; the results run next week, and the meeting video is on TV21 the day after.

A fiber franchise for AT&T's network was on the same agenda

AT&T bought most of Lumen's fiber business and, the city's agenda bill says, intends to expand fiber service in Sammamish.
