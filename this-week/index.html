What the city decided and what changed

The council skipped the city's 2027 property tax increase

On November 17 it voted a $0 increase beyond new construction and banked the 1% state law allows, $297,237.

The 2027–28 budget passed: $412.5 million over two years

General Fund spending is $160.4 million across both years, and the budget takes effect November 28.

New homes pay a different school impact fee from January 1, by district

A new single-family home with three or more bedrooms pays $8,810.25 in the Lake Washington district and $25,876.84 in Snoqualmie Valley; in the Issaquah district every home type pays $0.

On the calendar

King County transfer stations closed on Thanksgiving

Nov 26 · All day · King County solid waste facilities, including the Factoria Recycling and Transfer Station. King County offices and its transfer stations and recycling facilities close on Thanksgiving Day; garbage pickup changes come from your own hauler. Organizer's page
