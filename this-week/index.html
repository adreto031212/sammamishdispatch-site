What the city decided and what changed

Five public hearings are on the council's Tuesday, November 17 meeting, which starts at 6:30 PM

The city's notices list the 2027–28 budget and property tax levy (with potential adoption), the 2027–2032 Six-Year CIP, the Building 120 map change, the school districts' capital facilities plans…

The school fee hearing adopts three districts' plans, not just two

The council will consider three ordinances adopting the 2026–2031 capital facilities plans of the Issaquah, Lake Washington and Snoqualmie Valley school districts.

Volunteers plant a pollinator meadow at Evans Creek Preserve on Saturday, November 14, 11 AM to 2 PM

Crews will plant bulbs and wildflowers next to the existing pollinator site, part of the city's Bee City work.

where the generator goes

If you run a generator in an outage, keep it outdoors and more than 20 feet from windows, doors and vents, and never in the garage, even with the door open.
