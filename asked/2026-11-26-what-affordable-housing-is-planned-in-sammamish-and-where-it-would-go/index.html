What affordable housing is planned in Sammamish, and where it would go

November 26, 2026 · Sammamish Dispatch, issue 8 · Every answered question

A reader in Pine Lake asked what affordable housing is planned in Sammamish, and where it would go. What the city has is a requirement to make room, not a list of buildings. King County assigns Sammamish 2,100 homes at lower incomes: 499 permanent supportive housing units and 950 more for households under 30% of area median income (under $40,380 for four people), 419 under 50% ($67,300) and 232 under 80% ($107,680), plus 401 emergency housing units counted separately. The city must show zoning that fits them, and state guidance counts only low- and mid-rise buildings, the lowest incomes with subsidies. Where: the four centers (Town Center, Inglewood, Pine Lake, Klahanie) and publicly or religiously owned "bonus" parcels, with room for about 3,230 such homes on paper. None is a proposal. Planning Commission packet, pp. 7–8, 13
