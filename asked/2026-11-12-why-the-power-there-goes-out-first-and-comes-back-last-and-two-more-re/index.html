Why the power there goes out first and comes back last, and two more readers asked the same about parts of Sammamish

November 12, 2026 · Sammamish Dispatch, issue 6 · Every answered question

A reader south of Pine Lake asked why the power there goes out first and comes back last, and two more readers asked the same about parts of Sammamish. We found no public outage record by street or circuit for Sammamish, so we cannot answer for your block. PSE's own documents explain the pattern. Crews restore hospitals and other essential services first, then the high-voltage lines that feed substations, and only then the lines that serve homes. Trees are the big factor: on the circuits serving Bellevue in 2024, storms aside, trees caused about one outage in five but half of all outage minutes. After the November 2024 storm, PSE named Pine Lake, Klahanie and Sahalee among the areas still dark. PSE writes a yearly circuit-by-circuit report for Bellevue; we found none for Sammamish.
