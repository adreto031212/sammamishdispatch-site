Thu Nov 19 · 5 to 6:30 PM; tree lit at 5:15 PM

Issaquah Highlands Holiday Tree Lighting

Mayor Mark Mullet lights the big tree, with carolers, Santa and Mrs. Claus, and police and fire crews on hand.

Village Green Park, 2550 NE Park Dr · Free; free cocoa, cider and cookies · Organizer's page

Thu Nov 26 · All day

King County transfer stations closed on Thanksgiving

King County offices and its transfer stations and recycling facilities close on Thanksgiving Day; garbage pickup changes come from your own hauler.

King County solid waste facilities, including the Factoria Recycling and Transfer Station · None · Organizer's page

Sat Dec 5 · 10 AM to 2 PM

Lake Sammamish Holiday Market

The first holiday market in the park, with local makers, seasonal treats and native living trees to adopt.

Meadow Shelter, Lake Sammamish State Park · Free entry; Discover Pass required to park; living trees by donation ($100 for 2 ft, $200 for 4 ft) · Organizer's page

Fri Dec 11 · 5 PM (page lists both 7 PM and 8 PM as the end)

Frosty Fest 2026: Movie in a Winter Wonderland

The city's family evening in pajamas: a holiday movie, popcorn and cocoa, an ornament craft, Frozen characters and a food truck.

Building 120 (the former Central Washington University building) · Free · Organizer's page
