who handles what when a storm hits

October 22, 2026 · Sammamish Dispatch, issue 3 · All Sammamish news

Wind and rain bring down trees and power lines this time of year. City crews clear debris on city-owned land and streets; a tree that falls on private property is the owner's to remove. Power lines belong to the utility, so report outages to PSE and stay well clear of any downed line, assuming it is live. Keep extra food, water, flashlights and spare batteries on hand, and sign up for Alert King County for emergency notices. City of Sammamish · Alert King County
