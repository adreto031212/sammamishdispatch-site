November 2, not October 31

October 12, 2026 · Sammamish Dispatch, issue 1 · All Sammamish news

The second half of 2026 King County property tax is due Monday, November 2. The usual date is October 31; when it falls on a weekend, the deadline moves to the next business day. Mail a check postmarked by the due date to King County Treasury, 201 S. Jackson St., Suite 710, Seattle, WA 98104, with your account number on it. If a mortgage company pays from escrow, the county says to call that company if the payment still isn't showing ten days past the due date. Questions: 206-263-2890. King County Treasury · FAQ
