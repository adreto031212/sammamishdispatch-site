What changed in Sammamish.

Every story the Sammamish Dispatch has run, newest first. Each links the public record it came from.

November 12, 2026

Five public hearings are on the council's Tuesday, November 17 meeting, which starts at 6:30 PM

The city's notices list the 2027–28 budget and property tax levy (with potential adoption), the 2027–2032 Six-Year CIP, the Building 120 map change, the school districts' capital facilities plans…

The school fee hearing adopts three districts' plans, not just two

The council will consider three ordinances adopting the 2026–2031 capital facilities plans of the Issaquah, Lake Washington and Snoqualmie Valley school districts.

Volunteers plant a pollinator meadow at Evans Creek Preserve on Saturday, November 14, 11 AM to 2 PM

Crews will plant bulbs and wildflowers next to the existing pollinator site, part of the city's Bee City work.

where the generator goes

If you run a generator in an outage, keep it outdoors and more than 20 feet from windows, doors and vents, and never in the garage, even with the door open.

November 5, 2026

The council's budget and levy hearings run to November 17

The budget page lists a public hearing on the 2027–28 budget and property tax levy at a November 4 special meeting, a November 5 placeholder for budget review, and a November 17 regular meeting with…

The Six-Year capital plan hearing is also November 17

The city's legal notices list a public hearing on the 2027–2032 Six-Year CIP document on the same night.

A November 17 hearing is set on file POL2025-00572, tied to Building 120

A notice of public hearing dated November 3 carries that file number and the name Building 120.

Stormwater pipe cameras are inspecting lines through Friday, November 6

Crews are working at 228th Avenue SE and SE 8th Street, SE 22nd Place, 210th Place SE, 212th Avenue SE and NE 26th Place.

a freeze plan before the cold

Before a hard freeze, open the cabinet doors under sinks on exterior walls so room heat reaches the pipes, and let an indoor faucet drip cold water.

October 29, 2026

Second-half property taxes are due Monday, November 2

October 31 is a Saturday this year, so the deadline moves to the next business day.

Ballots for the November 3 general election are due by 8 PM Tuesday

Mail one postmarked by Election Day, or use a Vote Center or one of King County's 100 official drop boxes, which close at 8 PM sharp.

The budget's first public hearing is Wednesday, November 4

The special meeting takes testimony on the 2027–28 budget and the property tax levy, after this week's department presentations.

Three lane-closure jobs end this week

Tree trimming on 228th Avenue, Issaquah-Pine Lake Road to Sahalee Way, runs through Friday, October 30, as does stormwater-pipe camera work at 228th Avenue SE and SE 8th Street, SE 22nd Place and…

Issaquah elementary students have no school November 4 and 5

Those are conference days.

clocks back, alarms tested

Daylight saving time ends at 2 AM Sunday, November 1, when clocks fall back an hour.

October 22, 2026

The proposed 2027–28 budget spends $220.3 million against $204.0 million in revenue

The $16.3 million gap comes from savings, almost all for one-time capital projects; no new debt is planned.

Council goes through the budget department by department October 27 and 28

Both special meetings run 6:30 to 9 PM at City Hall and open with public comment.

Building 120 goes to a council hearing November 17

The change would make the 21.5-acre city site at 120 228th Ave NE a Mixed Use Center, opening a rezone from R-6 to Community Business: up to 18 homes an acre instead of 6, and 60 feet of height…

SE 32nd Street gets a new sidewalk and bike lane

For about eight weeks from October 19, crews build 630 feet of sidewalk and bike lane on the north side between 241st and 244th Avenues SE, weekdays, with flaggers.

The October 31 recycling list is out

At Eastside Catholic School, 9 AM to 3 PM: up to six passenger tires free, electronics, TVs, and lead-acid and alkaline batteries.

who handles what when a storm hits

Wind and rain bring down trees and power lines this time of year.

October 15, 2026

Building 120 reaches the council Tuesday, after tonight's Planning Commission hearing

The commission takes testimony at 6:30 PM on the map change for the 21.5-acre city site at 120 228th Ave NE. The council's long-range calendar puts a briefing at its 5:30 PM work session October 20…

New homes in the Lake Washington district would pay $8,810.25 in school impact fees from January 1, down from $10,914.75

That rate covers single-family homes with three or more bedrooms; under the new bedroom-count formula, every other Lake Washington category drops to $0, and the Issaquah district stays at $0.

A 9.27-acre rezone off SE 1st Street goes to the hearing examiner Friday, October 23

Owners of four parcels at 214th Ave SE and SE 1st Street ask to move from R-1 (one home per acre) to R-4 (four per acre); city staff recommend approval.

The fall recycling and shredding event is Saturday, October 31, 9 AM to 3 PM, at Eastside Catholic School, 232 228th Ave SE

It is free; items with fees are cash only, and no vehicles enter after 3 PM. The city says the accepted-materials list is published closer to the date.

leaves belong in the cart, not the drain

Sammamish has about 12,000 storm drains, and fall leaves are what clog them.

October 12, 2026

Tree crews take one lane of 228th Avenue, 9:30 AM to 3:30 PM, from today through October 30

The work runs both directions from Issaquah-Pine Lake Road to the city limit on Sahalee Way; the city says to expect delays.

Building 120 gets a land-use hearing Thursday

The Planning Commission meets at 6:30 PM on October 15 in Council Chambers, City Hall, 801 228th Ave SE, and streams online.

The 2027–28 budget arrives October 20

The city manager presents the proposed two-year budget at the 6:30 PM council meeting that Tuesday; special budget sessions follow October 27 and 28, and the budget and property tax levy hearings are…

No school Friday in Lake Washington schools; Issaquah schools are in session

Lake Washington School District has a LEAP day on October 16, and preschool programs pause October 13–15 for conferences.

Weekend work on East Lake Sammamish Parkway, every Saturday and Sunday through October 31

Crews on the George Davis Creek fish-passage project work 7 AM to 6 PM south of NE 7th Court, with a 20 mph limit and a posted detour map.

November 2, not October 31

The second half of 2026 King County property tax is due Monday, November 2.
