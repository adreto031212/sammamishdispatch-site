New homes in the Lake Washington district would pay $8,810.25 in school impact fees from January 1, down from $10,914.75

October 15, 2026 · Sammamish Dispatch, issue 2 · All Sammamish news

That rate covers single-family homes with three or more bedrooms; under the new bedroom-count formula, every other Lake Washington category drops to $0, and the Issaquah district stays at $0. The city collects the fees for the districts; council holds hearings and votes November 17. October 6 work session packet
