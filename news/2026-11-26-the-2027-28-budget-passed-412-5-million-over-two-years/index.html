The 2027–28 budget passed: $412.5 million over two years

November 26, 2026 · Sammamish Dispatch, issue 8 · All Sammamish news

General Fund spending is $160.4 million across both years, and the budget takes effect November 28. The six-year capital plan for 2027 to 2032 passed the same night; it is a planning document, not spending authority, and your neighborhood's lines are below.
